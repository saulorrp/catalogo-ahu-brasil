2. How the search works

The search combines three engines and merges their results by the rank of each document in each engine, not by an invented score. It searches the Portuguese summaries, so queries in Portuguese work best; the search by meaning also accepts English, with less precision.

  • Shelfmark. If the query is a shelfmark, in any of its three forms (Resgate, reference code or old shelfmark), the answer is the document with that shelfmark and nothing else. If the shelfmark appears in the middle of a phrase, that document comes first and the rest of the phrase follows the normal search.
  • Words. Looks for the words of the query in the summary and in the fields of the document and ranks the results by the frequency and rarity of each term. The fields have weights: document type 1.5, summary 1.0 and folder 0.5. Old spellings and accents are normalised (ph→f, y→i). Quotation marks ask for the exact phrase.
  • Meaning (semantic search). Compares the meaning of the query with that of each summary, using the multilingual model multilingual-e5-small. It finds documents that deal with the subject in other words. Strictness sets how far below the closest document a result may still enter.

On our own server there is a fourth step: a reranker (the multilingual cross-encoder mmarco-mMiniLMv2-L12) reads the query and each of the top 30 summaries together and reorders that top. That is why the list appears first and, a moment later, the order at the top may change. It neither removes nor adds documents, and it does not apply to the Exact mode or to sorting by year.

3. What the catalogue has, and what it does not

Each document carries the summary from the AHU catalogue as it was published, the three forms of shelfmark, the date, the folder (captaincy or region) and the document type (letter, ofício, petition…), which is the first word of the entry, normalised and flagged when it is rare in the catalogue. Errors in the source are not corrected: they get a [sic] on display. Folder names and document types come from the data and stay in Portuguese.
