References and sources

Arquivo Histórico Ultramarino (AHU)

Archive in Lisbon, founded in 1931, that holds the records of the Portuguese overseas administration. The core used here is the fonds of the Overseas Council (Conselho Ultramarino, 1642–1833), the body that dispatched colonial affairs: petitions, ofícios, consultations, letters from governors and town councils. The loose documents are arranged by series (usually one captaincy), box (caixa) and document, and that is what the shelfmark records. Today the AHU is part of the Direção-Geral do Livro, dos Arquivos e das Bibliotecas (DGLAB).

  • AHU website: https://ahu.dglab.gov.pt/
  • AHU catalogue in DigitArq, searchable by reference code (PT/AHU/CU/…): https://digitarq.ahu.arquivos.pt/

AHU catalogues in the ACTD

The Arquivo Científico Tropical Digital (ACTD), of the former Instituto de Investigação Científica Tropical (IICT, now part of the University of Lisbon), publishes the catalogues of the loose documents of the Overseas Council, series by series, as text PDFs. It is the main source of the summaries and shelfmarks of the ahu-brasil collection.

  • ACTD: https://actd.iict.pt/
  • Record of a series: https://actd.iict.pt/view/actd:CUc<series> (e.g. actd:CUc015, Pernambuco)

Projeto Resgate "Barão do Rio Branco"

Brazilian project, created in the 1990s, that located, catalogued and microfilmed the records on colonial Brazil held in foreign archives, above all in the AHU. Its catalogues, one per captaincy, give each document the shelfmark AHU_ACL_CU_… used in this app. The microfilm images were digitised and are online.

  • Project page: https://www.gov.br/bn/pt-br/central-de-conteudos/projeto-resgate
  • Digitised documents (microfilms): https://resgate.bn.gov.br/docreader/docmulti.aspx?bib=resgate. The server often refuses access from outside Brazil.

Biblioteca Nacional (BN)

The Fundação Biblioteca Nacional, in Rio de Janeiro, maintains the Projeto Resgate portal and the digitised images. Its Hemeroteca Digital holds the Anais da Biblioteca Nacional, where Castro e Almeida's inventory of the documents on Brazil in the former Arquivo de Marinha e Ultramar was published, the basis of the "Castro e Almeida" series for Bahia and Rio de Janeiro.

  • Biblioteca Nacional: https://www.gov.br/bn/pt-br
  • BN Digital: https://bndigital.bn.gov.br/
  • Hemeroteca Digital: https://memoria.bn.gov.br/

Mapping the Atlantic Portuguese Empire (MAPE)

Project that transcribed the AHU catalogues published in the ACTD and translated them into English. The English translation shown in this catalogue is the one in the MAPE dataset, reproduced unchanged.

  • Błoch, A.; Vasques Filho, D.; Bojanowski, M.; Santana, C.; Hussain, S.; Rzońca, A.; Podsiadły, A. MAPE: A Dataset of Correspondence from the Portuguese Empire, version 6, 2026. Zenodo. doi:10.5281/zenodo.24403753 (all versions: doi:10.5281/zenodo.15336798). CC BY 4.0.
  • Błoch, A.; Vasques Filho, D.; Bojanowski, M. Networks from archives: reconstructing networks of official correspondence in the early modern Portuguese empire. Social Networks, v. 69, p. 123–135, 2022. doi:10.1016/j.socnet.2020.08.008.

The three forms of shelfmark

  • Resgate shelfmark (current code): AHU_ACL_CU_017, Cx. 69, D. 6420. Series 017 (Rio de Janeiro), box 69, document 6420.
  • Reference code: PT/AHU/CU/017/0069/06420. The same document, in the DigitArq standard.
  • Old shelfmark: AHU-Rio de Janeiro, cx. 76, doc. 41. The numbering before Resgate, found in older works. It can be converted only through the database, because the numbering changed.
